▶ Visit Pathshala on YouTube

Teaching is a great way of learning!

Pathshala is my personal platform to grow as a teacher and become a better science communicator. Through this initiative, I aim to share ideas, concepts, and problems from different areas of science.

The goal is not only to explain concepts, but also to explore how we understand, solve, and apply them. I believe that teaching and learning go hand in hand, and that explaining an idea clearly is often one of the best ways to understand it more deeply. Preparing an explanation, solving a problem, or creating a computational demonstration often leads to a deeper understanding of the subject itself.

I hope this initiative gradually grows into a useful collection of educational resources and reaches a wider audience. If even a small part of the content helps someone understand a concept, solve a problem, or become curious about science, the effort will be worthwhile.

Pathshala will gradually expand beyond video-based learning. As the initiative grows, I plan to introduce other forms of learning, including self-learning courses and structured educational resources that learners can follow at their own pace.

The long-term vision is to build a flexible learning platform where concepts can be explored through videos, problems, computational exercises, courses, and other learning resources.
